## Watch list (below the top 12, reviewed monthly)

- **Solid skills or reactivity bugs:** S1b thresholds; more than 3 lint-missed reactivity bugs per member per phase triggers the React fallback before W4, or a new record afterwards. Owner R3.
- **Viva readiness and over-reliance on AI assistants:** a member cannot explain their PR, or mock viva scores are below the bar. Mitigation: the "explain your PR" rule, teach-backs and honest AI_USAGE.md entries. Owner R4 (AI_USAGE curator).
- **Memory accounting error:** calibration error above 25% against `measureUserAgentSpecificMemory`. Mitigation: refit the constants and report the error honestly. Owner R1.
- **Pinned versions going stale:** Node 24 moves to maintenance on 2026-10-20; PG 18.7 (the image digest is bumped only after all suites pass); TS 7.1. Pre-approved moves go in a new record. Owner R5.
- **Secret leak:** gitleaks fires. The runbook is: revoke, rotate, redeploy, then log it in SECURITY.md. Owner R4.
